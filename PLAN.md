# Plan

Use this file for large or multi-session work. Keep it short and current.

## Goal

Build a working prototype for one challenge of the "AI for Access" hackathon by Powercoders x Tech & Beyond. The event is in Bern on 20-21 November 2026. The team chooses the challenge at the event on Friday evening.

Source: [challenge preferences](https://hackathon.powercoders.org/en/preferences) and [challenges](https://hackathon.powercoders.org/en/#challenges).

## Challenges

| # | Challenge | Offered by | Problem | Tags |
|---|-----------|------------|---------|------|
| 1 | Beyond the List | PostFinance AG | A bank app shows more than transactions and helps users make better decisions. | AI/ML, software, data, UX |
| 2 | Enter Once, Publish Everywhere | Quartierzentrum Union | Events are entered by hand two or three times. Enter them once in Outlook and publish them automatically on the community centre site and the umbrella association site. | AI/ML, software, UX |
| 3 | Integration Cockpit | Stadt Bern, Asylsozialdienst | A cockpit shows which support (language, work, housing) helps refugees reach their integration goals, so caseworkers can help more people. | AI/ML, software, data, UX, law |
| 4 | Market Radar | Powercoders | A tool shows a non-technical team where the Swiss IT job market goes, which skills employers want and which policy changes matter. | AI/ML, software, data, UX |
| 5 | RightSide - Know Where to Stand | private person | Newcomers to Switzerland find the right bus stop and the right side of the road, in any language. | AI/ML, software, data, UX |
| 6 | Sats, Not Savings | SayNode Operations AG | An app holds rental deposits safely in Bitcoin, explains the process with AI and can give tenants back more than they paid. | AI/ML, software, UX, law |
| 7 | Triage Tool for Integration Services (Lucerne) | KUNIGO | A public tool guides people with a migration background in Lucerne to the right advice centre or service. | AI/ML, software, data, UX |

## Scope

- In: one challenge, chosen at the event; a working demo for the final presentation.
- Out: to be decided when the challenge is chosen.

## Approach

1. Before the event, each team member ranks the challenges on the preferences page.
2. On Friday evening, choose the challenge with the team.
3. Break the challenge into tasks, with one branch and one pull request per task.
4. Build the demo and update `PROGRESS.md` at the end of each work block.

## Risks / open questions

- The preferences page shows no deliverables and no judging criteria. Check the event schedule and the challenge briefs.
- The full challenge briefs are not in this repo yet.
- The tech stack is open until the challenge is chosen.
